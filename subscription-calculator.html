How the calculator works

Every plan is turned into a monthly cost: weekly plans × 52 ÷ 12, quarterly ÷ 3, every six months ÷ 6 and yearly ÷ 12. The yearly total is the monthly total × 12, and the daily figure is the yearly total ÷ 365.

Why add them up?

Subscriptions are small one by one: a video service, music, cloud storage, an AI assistant, a gym. Together they are often one of the larger fixed costs in a budget, and free trials quietly turn into paid plans. Seeing the yearly number makes it easier to decide what to keep.

Is my list saved or sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs in your browser. Your list is kept only on this device so it's there when you come back; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
