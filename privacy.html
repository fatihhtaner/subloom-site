Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Subloom ("the app") is built to keep your information on your own device. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data you enter

The subscriptions you add (names, prices, billing dates, notes and settings) are stored only on your device. If iCloud sync is turned on, this data is also stored in your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We, the developer, cannot access it.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Subloom does not require an account, does not connect to your bank, does not show ads, does not use analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Exchange rates

To convert prices between currencies, the app downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) about once a day. The request contains no personal data or subscription information. As with any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address.

Reminders

Payment reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Subloom Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and unlock Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history for Subloom, and basic device and app information. It does not receive your name, email address or the subscriptions you track.

Children

Subloom is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a subscription in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
