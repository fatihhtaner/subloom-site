Frequently asked questions

Does Subloom connect to my bank?

No. You add subscriptions yourself, and everything stays on your device.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open Settings → Notifications → Subloom on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. You can choose when reminders arrive in Subloom → Settings → Reminders.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Subloom → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro.

How do I cancel Subloom Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Subloom.
